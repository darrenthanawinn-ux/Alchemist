THE ALCHEMIST'S CHRONICLE
Open index.html in Chrome/Edge/Firefox (needs internet for the Three.js CDN). Click Play to lock the mouse.
Controls: WASD, Shift sprint, Ctrl crouch, Space jump/double-jump/glide, G grapple, E harvest/interact, LMB laser, B build/craft (1-6), V camera, K save, Esc pause.
Progress autosaves to localStorage every 15s.
